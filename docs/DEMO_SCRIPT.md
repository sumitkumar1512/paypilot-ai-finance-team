# 90-second demo script

1. Sign in with the provided demo credentials.
2. Start on Overview: point out revenue, 30-day transaction volume and success rate.
3. Show the Revenue Trend and Payment Health card. Explain that failures rose from the historic rate to the current rate, with UPI and the afternoon window identified automatically.
4. Open Receivables and select TechCorp. Generate and send its AI-written reminder.
5. Ask: **Why did revenue fall this week?** Point out the tool label under the response. The answer was composed after the backend executed revenue and failed-payment functions; it is not a generic chatbot response.
6. Ask: **Who owes me the most money?** Explain the outstanding and overdue total.
7. Close with: “PayPilot tells a founder what changed, why it happened, and what action to take.”
